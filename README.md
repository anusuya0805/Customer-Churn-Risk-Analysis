# Customer Churn Risk Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

Customer churn is an important business problem because losing existing customers can affect revenue, customer relationships, and long-term business growth.

This project analyzes historical customer data to understand churn patterns and identify customer groups that show higher levels of churn.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to explore churn by customer characteristics such as tenure, contract type, services, payment method, and other available customer attributes.

The project focuses on transforming customer data into clear analytical insights that can support customer retention and business decision-making.

---

## Problem Statement

Businesses need to understand why customers leave and which customer groups have higher churn levels.

Without structured analysis, it can be difficult to identify important churn patterns across different customer segments.

The problem addressed in this project is:

> **How can customer data be analyzed to identify churn patterns and customer segments with higher churn levels?**

The analysis uses historical customer data to identify patterns and areas that may require further investigation.

---

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard analyzes customer churn from multiple perspectives.

### Churn by Tenure

Customer churn is compared across different tenure groups to understand whether newer or longer-term customers show different churn patterns.

### Churn by Contract Type

Different contract types are compared to identify variations in churn levels.

### Churn by Services

Customer service and subscription characteristics are analyzed to understand their relationship with churn.

### Churn by Payment Method

Payment methods are compared to identify differences in customer churn patterns.

### Customer Segmentation

Customers are analyzed across different characteristics to identify groups with relatively higher churn levels.

---

## Key Insights

The analysis identified several patterns in the available dataset:

- Customers with shorter tenure showed higher churn levels.
- Month-to-month contract customers showed higher churn compared with customers on longer-term contracts.
- Certain service and payment-method segments showed relatively higher churn.
- Customer characteristics can be used to identify segments that require further investigation.
- Churn analysis can help businesses focus retention efforts on specific customer groups rather than treating all customers equally.

These findings describe patterns in the dataset and should not be interpreted as proof that a particular factor directly causes customer churn.

## Limitations

This project is based on the available historical customer dataset and the variables included in it.

The analysis does not capture every factor that may influence customer churn, such as:

- Individual customer circumstances
- Competitor offers
- Customer service experiences not recorded in the dataset
- Marketing campaigns
- Economic conditions
- Changes in customer preferences
- Future customer behavior

Therefore, the dashboard should be treated as an analytical and decision-support tool rather than a system that guarantees whether an individual customer will churn.
